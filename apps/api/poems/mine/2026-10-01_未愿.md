# 未愿

清江河畔，杨柳垂眉
闲捻笔墨，忽成信笺

目之所及，不见琼华
心之所念，天各一方
云卷飞逝，不共晚霞

露水风尘，千般情丝
念念不见，万物刍狗

回忆渐稀，不过夜半
愿君所愿，思君所思
唯盼荏苒，金风玉露


杨柳在河畔低垂，
我闲来执笔墨，
却字字都成思念。

目光所及之处，不见你的身影
心之所念的人，总是天各一方
天上云卷云舒，我们看的却总不是同一方云霞

露与水与风与尘，千万世间物都让我更思念你
思念却又不相见，千万世间物都让我不愿予仁义

回忆日渐稀疏，已经不够撑过夜半
我祈愿着你的愿望，思索着你的思绪
只盼着时间匆匆过去，可以终于与你重相逢


By the riverbank,
the willows bend low.
In an idle hour, I take up brush and ink—
yet every word becomes a thought of you.

Wherever my gaze may fall,
there is no trace of you.
The one my heart turns to
is always beneath another sky.
Above us, clouds gather and unfold,
yet the clouds we watch at dusk
are never the same.

Dew and water, wind and dust—
the myriad things of this world
only make me long for you more.
Longing, yet never meeting,
I find no kindness left to give
to all the myriad things beneath heaven.

Memories grow thinner with each passing day,
no longer enough to carry me beyond midnight.
I pray for all that you wish for,
and linger over every thought of yours.
I ask only that time hasten by,
until at last
I may meet you once more.
